Freedom

“@♫⃠ Im a friggin deer ♫⃠: Imagine waking up like this and going outside to hunt dinner for a few weeks… ahhhhh the freedom”

Well thank you for the short message mr I’m a friggen dear. But now you can hop in the passenger seat and buckle up, because it’s my turn behind the wheel.

There’s a spectrum, on one side a quiet life of solitude free from the worries of modern life, knock down the wall and on the other side a buzzing vibrant life filled with people free from the worries of a modern life.

Both approaches try to combat the struggles the every day man is thrown. Like a ship in a tempest, it can either batten down its hatches, or rev the engine and unfurl its sails.

The never ending cycle, jormungandr gnawing on its tail like a dog on a bone. Until the dog is no longer interested. The bone is devoid of any nutrients. Ragnarok has occurred.

The human mind cannot be trapped for so long. Eventually, even for a bit, it must spread its wings. Open up the golden cage, take a leap and fly free. Wether physically, or mentally.

Cog in the machine this, cog in the machine that. Does one ever wonder why such a phrase is uttered so much? The human psyche yearns to leave its stress behind, which can be obtained in many ways but for the simplicity in this two dimensional approach. I will not be switching my tv to colour, the blindfold is on and I am looking the other way. No, this is a 2d sequence, a line. Point A. Point B. And all the space between it. Only point A is Point B and vice versa, but at the same time they are not.

Point A attempts to achieve this goal by looking for total solitude. The crackle of a fire, the sweet prickly smell of pine filling your nostrils, and the stars illuminating the forest around you. Doesn’t this life sound nice? No people, no work, no stress… just you standing over a crystal clear lake, your ole dog Betsy curled up by your legs, Remington on your back, and your rod thwipping through the air.

Equilibrium. Balance. Balanced. Perfectly. As all things should be. On the other side of the scale comes point B. Flip the record over and see what’s on side B. The 4 P’s are on the other side. People, partying, pmusic, pdrugs. “Ja is dis da discotek?” It certainly is. The bass is pumping, emotions are high. You are high. Neon lights fill the sky, reminiscing of shinjuku. Stinky smelly sweat fills your nostrils, a bald man moving in ways you never thought were possible. And the dissonant hum of feet stomping competing with the giant speakers towering over you. Your brain is free, clear, no thoughts, no worries, just music.

If you were to pick a point from 1-100 what would you pick? No seriously, think. What would you pick

It certainly isn’t 1.

It certainly isn’t 100.

And that’s okay.

Now I’m going to continue having you imagining. I know, all the work what a bore. Look back into history, long long ago. The simple man lived in the woods with his fellow wild men, frisky. Fishing, hunting, gathering, chatting. Was his life okay? No. It was full of stress. Where is my next meal? Yada yada yada. The pendulum clicks on either side of the clock BUT that is only because there is a middle to pass through. Without a point of comparison, picking either side of the spectrum is useless.

Ding dong. Ding dong. The school bell rings and there is little Timmy at his desk! Now what was little Timmy doing all last night… oh that’s right I remember. Analyzing English work. Looking over fitzgerald’s BEAUTIFULLLL story on how amazing alcohol and partying is and trying to find the authors secret message. Now I could give you a where’s Waldo and have you hunting through for a hidden message in mine, but I want to hit the ball out of the park on my delivery ensuring you understand. You can pick through the text and look for more hidden meanings, but goodluck i riddled it with shitty metaphors, paradoxes and the such to befuddle anyone who dares to try, much like the noise of modern life I should add. You do not need to pick an extreme, you can fall anywhere on this spectrum. Or even off the spectrum if you want to be feisty about it. And that doesn’t matter. As long as you aren’t complacent in life and strive for more, jormungandr will never grab it’s tail again. And you will be waving your red flag and controlling the bull of life by your own choices.
